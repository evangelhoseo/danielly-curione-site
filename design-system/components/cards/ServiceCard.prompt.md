Rounded tile presenting one service/attendance type, used in 3-up grids on Home and service pages.

```jsx
<ServiceCard title="Terapia de casal" description="Um espaço para que os dois possam falar e ser escutados." />
<ServiceCard highlighted title="Não sabe por onde começar?" description="Escreva no WhatsApp." />
```

`highlighted` swaps the panel to the wood-tone surface and centers content — use for exactly one "soft CTA" card per grid, never for an actual service.
